CLEAN TECH JOKES

1. Why did the programmer bring a ladder to work?
Because the bug was on a higher level.

2. A database administrator walks into a bar and asks:
“Can I join you?”
The tables reply:
“Only if you have a key.”

3. Why do Java developers wear glasses?
Because they do not C#.

4. Two threads walk into a café.
The waiter asks, “Who was first?”
Both answer, “It depends on the scheduler.”

5. A programmer tells a computer:
“Give me a break.”
The computer replies:
“Sure. InterruptedException.”

6. Why was the function calm under pressure?
Because it always returned gracefully.

7. A developer tried to explain recursion to a friend.
The friend asked, “What is recursion?”
The developer said, “First, let me explain recursion.”

8. Why did the server go to sleep?
It had too many requests and needed some REST.

9. What did one byte say to the other?
“See you at the bus.”

10. Why did the concurrency book refuse to rush?
Because good synchronization takes time.
